How to use this map

  1. Move around: drag to pan, scroll or use + / − to zoom. Zoom in where markers overlap to separate them.
  2. Get details: click any pin or icon to open a popup. Borehole popups link to the available data.
  3. Find boreholes by data type: open Filters and tick the data you need. Only boreholes with all of it stay on the map.
  4. Change what is shown: open Base Layers to switch the basemap or hide layers.
  5. Not sure what a symbol means? Open Legend.

Solid Carbon Data Finder

Data compiled by Ocean Networks Canada from a variety of sources.
About this map

Explore places of interest to the Solid Carbon project across the Cascadia Basin and access variety of data compiled from ocean network databases for easy retrieval.

  • Markers — click a borehole for details and data links.
  • Filter Data Types — narrow markers to boreholes with a chosen data type.
  • Bathymetry opacity — fade the depth layer to see the basemap underneath.
  • Layers (top right of map) — toggle the basemap and bathymetry overlay.
